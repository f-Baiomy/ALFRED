Log DB sits next to Log inbound and works the same way: one switch per project, live, shared by every cycle and user.

The DB switch can only be on while inbound logging is on - statements are linked to inbound calls, so with inbound off there is nothing to attach them to. Turning inbound off greys the DB switch out (try it).

A cycle that is recording keeps the statements of its captured calls, like it keeps their bodies - deleting the cycle removes them.
