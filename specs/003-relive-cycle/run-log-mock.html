What changed from today's log

  • One row per step attempt. Today a call shows twice: the page's SENT line and the proxy's FORWARDED_LIVE line. They become one row. Request changes, rules, variables and holds open under it.
  • Rows in time order. Today the log lists events as they arrived, so a proxy line comes after the page line that followed it.
  • Short local time plus time since the run started. The full ISO timestamp moves to the hover. Today two formats are mixed (…19.967Z and …19.634926+00:00).
  • Words instead of codes: FORWARDED_LIVE becomes a LIVE badge, COMPLETED_WITH_DIFFERENCES becomes "≠ differs", (header, 200) becomes the HTTP column.
  • Path only, with a direction arrow: ↓ inbound into your app, ↑ outbound to a supplier. The host (host.docker.internal:9001) is in the hover and in the opened row.
  • Fixed columns on one line, so nothing wraps and the eye can scan straight down a column.
  • Problems = failed, differs, blocked or unexpected, with a red or amber edge. "Open step" jumps to the step in the timeline.
