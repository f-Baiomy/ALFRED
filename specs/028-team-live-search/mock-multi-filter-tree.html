Team search - every filter multi-select, and a tree view

Mock for review. Every filter in the Team tab's search picks several values (one shared picker, the same app-multi-select-picker the rule editor uses), each sent to Azure as an IN (...) or an OR group. The results can be shown as items only, with their parents, or as the full tree (parents and children). Click the pickers and the view switch; the panel on the right sets the states.
