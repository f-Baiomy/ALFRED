What changes

One header, then tabsThe board's title, next step, main item, cycle and stage bar stay on top. Everything else moves into tabs, so only one part shows at a time.
Cards firstCards is the default tab: the board you came for. The tab shows the open count.
CallsEvery call of every cycle on the board, in one list, newest first. Each row shows its cycle as a coloured tag, and a thin divider marks where one cycle's recording ends. Chips filter to one cycle, plus search, direction and "Problems only". A row opens the usual call popup. "Group by cycle" folds the list under each cycle instead. A cycle on several boards shows on each. Read on demand and refreshed by the calls signal, never polled.
TaskToday's Task panel (Requirements, Test plan, Brief, Claude's runs, Spec & files) becomes one tab, with its own sub-tabs. The tab shows what needs you ("no requirements", "2 to check").
Cycles, Related itemsMove out of the Task panel and the top strip into their own tabs.
ChatStays in the chat panel at the bottom, not in a tab. Opening a board puts its chat there folded (the tab beside the Chats pill, as in your screenshot) - even before its first message, so a new board's chat is always one click away. A click opens it; × closes it until the board is opened again. If you already have it open, it stays open. 💬 Chat in the header opens it too.
ActivityThe "Board activity" fold becomes a tab. The live strips (who answers, Claude stopped, new build) stay one line under the tabs.
RememberedThe tab is in the address (?tab=task), so a link or a reload opens the same tab. Old links without ?tab open Cards.
