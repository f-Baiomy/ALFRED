A thread = one comment and its replies (Alfred keeps one level of replies, as today). Five situations, today on the left, v3 on the right. Click "N more replies", "Reply", the question options and "edited".

Threads in v3

  • Replies sit under their comment on a thin rail, with smaller avatars - no box around each one, no repeated full timestamp.
  • Long threads fold in the middle: the first comment, "▸ 4 more replies", then the last two - the start and where it stands now.
  • "Reply…" line under each thread opens the reply box right there, quoting what you answer; Esc cancels; your draft is kept.
  • Claude's questions are a card inside the thread: each question with its options as buttons (★ = Claude's recommendation), "waiting for you" until answered, then ✓ with your pick. "Answer all" still opens the dialog; "Clear Needs decision" stays as a checkbox on the reply box.
  • ✅ on a Claude plan or question = your go-ahead, shown as "✅ go-ahead given" under it.
  • Edits: a small "edited" after the time opens the earlier texts.
  • Folded thread line (older, answered threads): "▸ You asked for the steps · 3 replies · last Claude 11:21 · ✅".

What v3 adds to v2

  • Journey line under the chips: ✓ Inbox Oct 14 › ✓ To do › ✓ In progress Oct 15 › ✓ Fixed › Verified ✦ proposed › ● Done today. Where the card is and how it got there, in one glance; click a step to move it there.
  • ‹ 3 / 12 › in the bar: previous / next card in the same column, without closing the drawer ([ and ]).
  • "2 new since you looked" bar at the top of Activity with Jump ↓ and Mark seen; new comments get a blue ring and dot; a dot on a tab whose content changed (Details: the description was edited).
  • 🔍 Find in the activity: filters the exchanges as you type and opens the ones that match.
  • Arabic and English side by side: each comment picks its direction from its own text (dir=auto) - an Arabic answer reads right-to-left with code still left-to-right; no more per-comment "⇄ Auto" button needed.
  • Code blocks with a header: what it is, ⧉ copy, fold - SQL and JSON in answers stay readable and short.
  • Quick replies above the comment box when Claude is waiting on you: ✅ Go ahead · ❓ Answer the questions · Explain more · Not now.
  • "✓ draft saved" in the comment box - your unsent text is kept per card (as today, now visible).
  • Keyboard: ? shows the list - 1–4 tabs, J/K exchanges, N new, / reply, E edit, [ ] cards.
  • Compact spacing switch in ⋯ for long cards on small screens.

Smart defaults

  • Needs you - open when something asks for your hand (proposal, questions, blocked, re-test failed), each with its button. Context only (looks like #31, reason) folds to "▸ 2 notes" inside it; alone, it shows as a closed "Notes" line. Nothing pending → no section.
  • Tab - Activity when there is a conversation, Details on a new card. Tab labels carry their state: ● Activity while Claude runs, 🔒 Links when blocked, ✓ 2/2 or ✗ 1/2 on Re-test. Needs-you rows jump to the right tab.
  • Description - open. Folded to 6 lines with "Read all" only when it is long.
  • Attached - open (one row of chips).
  • Exchanges - the last two open; older answered ones fold to one line. An exchange with an open question never folds.
  • Runs - one line; open while a run is going.
  • Links, Re-test - their own tabs, shown whole (no folding needed there).

Changes

  • Exchanges, not entries: your comment + Claude's answer (+ their replies) are one exchange. Older exchanges fold to one line: "You asked for the steps · Claude answered (3 steps) · ✅".
  • Day dividers (Yesterday, Today) instead of a timestamp on every comment; exact time on hover.
  • Changes become a dim one-liner between exchanges ("· 3 changes"), opened on click.
  • Answer first: Claude's first sentence shows bold; the rest (steps, SQL, evidence) under "Details ▸" when the answer is long.
  • View options (comments only, with changes, newest first) move into the conversation's ⋯ - no filter bar.

Reading

  • Text 14.5 px, line height 1.6, at most 68 characters wide - in Full screen the text no longer runs across 1,200 px.
  • Fewer boxes: no border around comments or sections, spacing and one hairline separate them; colour only for status, Claude and things that need you.
  • One accent (blue) for actions; green/amber/red only mean pass/needs you/fail.
  • Inline tags (REPLY, FOUND, NEXT) small and grey; code and endpoints in mono chips.
  • Empty means hidden: no "Nothing linked", "Not blocked", "No team yet" rows - the section line says it.
