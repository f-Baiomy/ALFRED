What changed, in one look

  • Header 9 rows → 3. One bar (number, kind, cycle, state pills, ▶ run, ⋯, view, ✕), the title, one line of properties.
  • Only what is set is shown. Flags that are off, empty teams, "Not blocked", "Nothing linked" no longer take rows - they live in "+ add" menus.
  • Things that need you come first - Claude's proposal, open questions, "looks like #31" - as one stack of callouts under the title, each with its buttons.
  • Body in tabs: Activity (default when there are comments), Details (description, pins, pages, files), Links, Re-test - each tab with a count.
  • Comments read like a conversation: avatar, name, time; Reply/React/Copy/direction appear on hover; long answers fold.
  • Comment box is one line until you click it; the 25-icon toolbar becomes 9 icons + "⋯ more" with the rest.
