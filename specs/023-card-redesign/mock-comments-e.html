Comments - simple waterfall

Every comment you can see is shown whole; what folds is the replies under a comment - from level 1 down.

1 · A reply sits right under what it answersA whole conversation stays together, so lines never cross another one.
2 · Each level steps rightEvery reply moves one small step (1 rem) right of what it answers - avatar and text together, so the gap between them is always the same. Every level steps, however deep. A thin line joins it to its parent.
3 · Comments are always shown; only replies foldEvery comment you can see shows its text. A long one is cut at about 9 lines with "▾ Show all", as today.
4 · All replies start folded, level 1 tooA thread opens as its first comment plus "▸ 5 replies · 1 new". Click it (or the line under the avatar) to open one level; each reply that has its own replies shows its own "▸ N replies".
