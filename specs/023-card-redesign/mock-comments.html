2c · Another threading design: boxed branches

No lines at all. Each reply is a soft box with a coloured edge for its author; a reply to it sits inside the box. Depth reads as boxes inside boxes, and every box folds from its own "▾ N replies" (click it). Calm to read, but each level costs a box edge and padding (~14 px), so deep threads get narrower faster than the tree.

What changes

Avatar beside the name, not a columnA smaller avatar sits at the start of the name line; the text starts under the name, 1.6 rem in.
No "REPLY" columnA part label that only says "Reply" is dropped. Meaningful ones (Done, Fixed, Correction, Question, Found) become a chip on the name line, so the text keeps the whole width.
Readable levelsCurved elbows tinted by author, about 22 px per level, at most 4 levels drawn (deeper replies say “↳ to …”). Hover lights one branch; ± folds a single branch to one line.
Replies as a treeA thin rail from the parent and an elbow per reply. A reply to a reply hangs under the reply it answers, with "↳ to “…”" when it is not the one right above.
Thread summary line“▾ 4 replies · You, Claude · last 12:05 PM” under the root: fold or unfold a thread, see who answered and whether something is new.
Did / Found / Next as blocksEach part gets a small heading line above its text instead of a label column. Found has an amber edge, Next sits in a box, and a folded thread shows its Next on the summary line.
Full-width tables and codeTables in a comment stretch to the text width instead of their content width.
UnchangedHover actions (☺ ↩ ⧉ ⋯), reactions, ✅ go-ahead, questions with Answer…, edit and history, new rings, "▸ N earlier replies", Reply… at the end of a thread.

One thing needs the backend: today a reply to a reply is stored pointing at the thread's first comment, so the tree has one level. To hang a reply under the reply it answers, the backend keeps the comment it was written to (a new nullable column) and the timeline nests by it. Older replies, without it, stay one level - as they are now.
