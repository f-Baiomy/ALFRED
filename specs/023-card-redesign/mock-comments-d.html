Comments - option D, in full (waterfall)

Every comment in time order at full width, like a chat. Nothing is indented. How a thread branches is shown in three quiet ways:

  • A quote on a reply, naming what it answers. It shows only when that comment is not the one right above, so a plain back-and-forth reads with no quotes at all.
  • A thin colour strip per branch.
  • Branch chips on the thread, which filter it to one conversation.

Waterfall: each reply's avatar steps one level to the right of the comment it answers, and a curved line joins them, coloured by conversation. Only the avatars step; the text column stays put, so a deep thread never squeezes the text. Past level 4 the step stops. Hover a comment to light its line up.

Collapsing: the ± under any avatar with replies folds its whole subtree into one line ("▸ 3 replies · You, Claude · last: “…” · 1 new"), which stays in the waterfall at the reply's level. Click that line, the ±, or the curved line itself to unfold. Folds inside a folded branch are remembered. The thread bar has ⊟ Fold branches (one line per conversation) and ⊞ Unfold all. In example 2 the Export conversation starts folded below “Yes - …:85”.

Try it: hover a quote to light up the comment it answers, click the quote to jump there and "↩ Back" to return, and click a branch chip to filter.

What option D does

Waterfall gutterAvatars step right by level (0.8 rem a step, at most 4) and curved lines join each reply to what it answers. The gutter is only as wide as the thread's deepest level: 2.1 rem for a plain thread.
Collapsing± on a comment, or a click on its line, folds everything under it into one summary line: how many, who, the last words, and how many are new. ⊟ Fold branches leaves one line per conversation. A new reply never folds itself away: its summary says "N new".
Full width, alwaysThe text never indents, at any depth. Text and tables use the whole width in the side panel and in full screen.
Quote only when neededA reply quotes what it answers only when that is not the comment right above. A plain back-and-forth shows no quotes.
Quote = jumpHovering a quote lights up the comment it answers. Clicking scrolls there and flashes it, "↩ Back" returns, and a quote into the hidden part opens that part.
Branches by colourEach conversation in a thread gets a thin colour strip. The thread's chips (● Export · 5) filter to one conversation.
Grouped follow-upsThe same person within a few minutes gets no second name line. The time shows on hover.
Parts quoted aloneReplying to the Next of a report quotes "Claude · Next", not the whole report.
Kept from todayHover actions ☺ ↩ ⧉ ⋯, reactions and ✅ go-ahead, questions with Answer…, edit and history, new rings and "N new since you looked", "▸ N earlier replies", Reply….
BackendQuotes need the comment a reply was written to. Today a reply to a reply is stored as a reply to the thread's first comment, so the backend keeps the real one in one new nullable column. Older replies just show no quote.
