Design B, B+ and B++

Each step keeps everything from the one before. Every idea is independent - you can also answer "B plus 1, 4, 9 and 12". All three have the timeline coloured by level, like the explorer's histogram.

B+ eight ideas on top of B

#IdeaWhy
1Turn a filter off without deleting it (the dot on the pill)Compare with and without a filter in one click.
2Each filter shows how many lines it hides ("−87")When the list goes empty you see which filter did it.
3"is any of" - several values in one pilllevel is ERROR, WARN in one filter.
4◀ ▶ step the range by its own lengthRead a log hour by hour.
5Around From: ± 1 min / ± 5 min / ± 1 h"What happened around this moment?"
6Recent rangesBack to the range you just looked at.
7Double-click a timeline bar selects that sliceJump straight into an error burst.
8"To = now, keep moving" and "N lines in this range" before ApplyLive range; know before applying if it's empty.

B++ seven more on top of B+

#IdeaWhy
9AND / OR between filters (click the "and" between two pills)level is ERROR or timeTaken > 6000 - without two searches.
10Undo / Redo (↶ ↷, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y)Experiment freely; get the previous setup back.
11Tick lines → "⏱ Use as time range" (optionally ± 1 min)"Everything between these two lines" in one click.
12Level chips above the list: ERROR 87 · WARN 15 · INFO 158The most common filter, one click, with counts.
13Search as text: copy it, edit it, paste one backShare exactly what you were looking at; power users can type it all.
14Keyboard: [ / ] previous / next window, / search, Ctrl+ZFast stepping through a log without the mouse.
15Timeline selection shows its start, end and line count while you dragNo need to look away to the From / To fields.
