Logs explorer - editable filters and a from / to time range

Mock with static lines (times stored in UTC, as your wildfly source). Click a filter pill to edit it, ⇄ to flip it, and the time button to pick a range. Lines outside the filters fade out so you can see the effect.

1Every filter pill can be edited: click it and it turns back into its text (message:API request, -level:ERROR, timeTaken>6000) in place - change it, Enter saves, Esc cancels, emptying it removes it. ⇄ flips a filter between "filter for" (=) and "filter out" (≠) without retyping.
2The time button opens a range picker: the quick ranges as before, plus From and To - each a date and a time down to the second (milliseconds if typed). Either side can be left empty ("from 10:00 onwards"). "Now" and "Newest line" fill a side in one click. Zooming on the histogram fills the same From / To.
3Which clock the times you type are in: Local (your computer, e.g. UTC+03:00 - converted to UTC before searching) or UTC (the source's own zone, the one the list shows - searched exactly as typed). The picker always shows what will actually be searched in UTC, so a 3-hour shift is never a surprise. The choice is remembered.
4To follows From: picking From sets To to the same moment, and To keeps following until you change To yourself - from then on they are independent.
5🔗 Keep the gap (between From and To): turn it on with, say, 21-3-2026 → 21-4-2026, and moving either side moves the other by the same amount - the range slides, its length stays one month. Turn it off to change one side alone.
6A clock picker for the time: click a time to open a dial - pick the hour (outer ring 1-12, inner ring 13-00), then the minutes, then the seconds; click or drag around the dial, or click HH / MM / SS at the top to jump back to one. "Now" fills the current time.
8Show times in UTC or Local: a switch in the Time column heading. It changes how every time on the page reads - the list, the histogram's edges, the line's own time fields, the range pill - and the header line ("times in UTC" / "times in local, UTC+03:00"). Hovering a time shows it in the other clock too. Nothing is searched differently: lines are still stored and searched in UTC; this is only how they are shown. The time picker starts in the same clock you are viewing, and the choice is remembered per source.
7The active range shows as a cyan pill in the search bar (time 2026-10-04 12:00:00 → 15:00:00 local) - click it to edit, ✕ to clear.
