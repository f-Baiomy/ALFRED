What changes
1The side drawer goes away. Its tabs move into the expanded line itself: Fields · Raw · Context · Trace. Comments stay on each field, and "Comment on the whole line" stays at the top. The ⇥ button on every row and "Open in drawer" are removed.
2A long value shows its first 3 lines, faded at the bottom, with its size beside the field name and two links under it: Show all (opens it in place, Show less folds it back) and ⤢ Open (the same text in its own window, with find and Copy).
3The value itself is never changed: same text, same order, same wrapping as today. Short values look exactly as they do now.
