Design B+ - design B with eight more ideas

Everything from B is unchanged. The NEW ideas below can each be taken or left:
1Turn a filter off without deleting it - the dot on each pill; it greys out and stops filtering, click again to turn it back on.
2Each filter says how many lines it hides ("−87"), so you see which one empties the list.
3"is any of" - click several values in the form (ERROR and WARN) for one pill: level is ERROR, WARN.
4◀ ▶ step the range by its own length - the previous / next 2 hours - from the time pill or the panel. Made for reading a log hour by hour.
5Around a moment: "± 5 min around From" (or ± 1 min, ± 1 h) - the usual question when a line looks wrong.
6Recent ranges: the last ranges you applied, one click away in the quick list.
7The timeline is coloured by level (errors red, warnings amber), and double-click a bar to select just that slice.
8"To = now, keep moving" - a live range that follows new lines; and the panel tells you how many lines the range holds before you apply it.

Static mock, lines stored in UTC. Click a pill to edit it, "+ Add filter" to add one, and the time button to open the range panel. Lines outside the filters fade out.

1Filters are edited in a small form, not as text: click a pill → Include / Exclude, Field (type any part of the name - matching fields are suggested as you type, with their type and how many lines have them), Condition (is, is not, contains, exists, missing, greater, less), Value with the field's most common values to click (and their counts). "Exclude" pills read NOT level is ERROR in red. Typing in the search bar still works for people who like it.
2One clock switch for the whole page (top right): UTC or Local - every time shown, typed and picked follows it, and each time in the range panel shows its other-clock twin underneath. No separate "times I type are" choice.
3Time range panel: quick ranges on the left (incl. Today, Yesterday); a calendar - click a start day, then an end day (days that have log lines get a dot); From / To times with ▲▼ per hour, minute and second (or type them, or scroll the mouse wheel on a number), plus a 🕒 button that opens a clock dial (hour ring, then minutes, then seconds) - both set the same time; "start of day / end of day / now" shortcuts; and a timeline of your data that zooms to your range (it shows the selection with a margin either side, so a 10-minute range is wide, not a sliver): drag anywhere on it to select, drag the edges to resize, drag the middle to slide it while keeping its length; the mouse wheel zooms, ⤢ Fit / All data jump; a thin overview strip above shows all your data and where the zoomed view sits (drag its box to move the view); hovering a bar shows its time and how many lines it holds.
4Type it in words: "yesterday 14:00 to 16:30", "last 90 minutes", "today", "2026-10-04 09:00 to 12:00" - it fills the calendar and times.
5The length is shown and can be locked ("2 h 30 min · 🔒 keep"), and To follows From until you set To, like design A.
