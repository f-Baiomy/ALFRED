Where each enhancement is in this mock (and why it stays quiet)
  1. Verdict timeline - click a card chip (#81) on a row
  2. Ready to re-test - the violet line above the list; ✕ hides it until another card is fixed
  3. Pause suggestion - on the recording row, once, when every card was checked; never pauses by itself
  4. Steps from the description - Overview, ticked by the calls; you can untick
  5. Story - the Story sub-tab, the failing step opened
  6. Diff vs baseline - "vs baseline" link on a row, Compare dialog
  7. Hot spots - Overview, click one to see its calls
  8. Ask about this cycle - the box at the bottom of Overview
  9. Claude's summary - Overview, only after a pause; edit or remove; off in ⋯ for good
  10. ⚑ Baseline - the tag on a row; set from ⋯
  11. Environment - the "env 80" tag and the Environment filter
  12. Archive - ⋯ Archive; "2 archived" at the bottom
  13. Evidence pack - ⋯ Evidence pack…
  14. Keyboard - ↑ ↓ Enter Space x, ? for the list
  15. Live counters - the recording row's calls count up as calls arrive
