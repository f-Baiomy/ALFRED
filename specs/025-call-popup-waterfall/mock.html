How it works: the popup body becomes Live Calls' own waterfall (app-call-waterfall, reused - not a copy): the call opened, its outbound calls under it (fetched with /calls/{id}/children, live or from its cycle), each a line with its bar on one time axis; a parent is bracketed by an opening and a closing line with own-work / waiting split. Click any line to open its full call card under it (the opened call's card starts open, every block shown as today). Every level is shown, however deep: each child's own children are fetched the same way until a call has none (a loop guard stops a call that names itself as its own ancestor). No children → one line and a full bar, so the popup always reads the same way.
