What Copy puts on the clipboard

Everything currently on screen, for the view you are in — status, headers and body, diff markers included. That is the thing worth pasting into a ticket, and it is what “copy all” has to mean; copying only the body would drop the status change that is usually the point. Switch to As upstream answered and Copy gives you that one side clean, with no markers, ready to replay.

Two limits, stated rather than discovered

Search covers what is on screen — the headers and the body of the current view — and steps through matches in reading order, so 3/7 means the third from the top of the panel, not the third in the body.

Past ~4,000 lines the diff stays monochrome. Colour is one DOM node per token instead of one per line; a 28,000-line SOAP body measured 186,734 nodes and a four-second freeze in the call view, which is why that view windows. Here the honest trade is plain text — still pretty-printed, still searchable, still copyable.
