Reading it: red is what was really there, green is what the other side actually got. On the request, red is what your app sent and green is what Sabre received. On the response, red is what Sabre really answered and green is what your app received. So “the booking failed” can be traced to status: FAILED being yours, not Sabre’s — Sabre said CONFIRMED.

Both sides are stored, not recomputed. A diff of two stored documents can always be shown; a diff against something that was never captured cannot.
